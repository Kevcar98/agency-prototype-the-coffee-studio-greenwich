THE ARCHITECTURE

THE GREENWICH STUDIO

Housed in a former industrial warehouse, our Greenwich location is a masterclass in functionalism. Raw concrete textures are balanced by warm timber accents and precision-engineered stainless steel equipment.

Our co-working facilities are designed for high-output individuals. We provide high-speed connectivity, ergonomic seating, and a specialized acoustic environment that minimizes distraction without sacrificing the vibrant energy of the cafe.
